主語言Lead

「一甕醬，等一百八十個日頭。」“One jar,a hundred and eighty suns.”

在西螺的曬場上，醬油還照阿公那一輩的做法：青仁黑豆蒸熟、長麴、下甕，一層豆一層鹽，封口交給日頭。

閱讀中文全文

半年後開甕，生醬再用龍眼木柴火慢煮六個鐘頭。「慢火」兩個字，是從灶口來的。

Most soy sauce starts with soybeans and wheat; this one is black beans and sea salt, brewed in clay jars in an open yard outside Xiluo.

Read in English

Expect dried plum and toasted rice. The fire in the name is the last step: longan wood, kept low for six hours.

六道工序Six steps, one of them half a year long

第一道：揀豆一日

青仁黑豆攤在竹篩上，破的、蛀的、顏色不對的一粒粒揀掉。

閱讀中文全文

一百斤豆大約揀出兩斤；篩底的豆皮留著，春天拿去堆肥。

1

Step 1: Sortingone day

Black beans are picked over by hand on bamboo sieves.

Read in English

Split or off-color beans come out, about two pounds in a hundred; the husks go to compost.

第二道：蒸豆一日

前一晚泡水，隔天上大灶，蒸到兩指一捏就扁。

2

Step 2: Steamingone day

Soaked overnight, then steamed until a bean gives between two fingers.

第三道：製麴七日

蒸好的豆攤進麴房，七天長滿黃綠色的麴花。

閱讀中文全文

天熱的晚上，三點得起來開窗。

3

Step 3: Growing kojiseven days

A week on bamboo trays in the koji room, until each bean wears a yellow-green coat.

Read in English

On warm nights the windows are opened at 3 a.m.

第四道：洗麴下甕一日

洗掉多餘麴花，一層豆一層鹽裝進陶甕，蓋上棉布與斗笠。

4

Step 4: Into the jarsone day

Rinsed, then layered with salt into clay jars under cloth and a bamboo hat.

第五道：日曬一百八十日

晴天掀蓋，變天就蓋。

閱讀中文全文

半年裡甕口棉布換三次、斗笠補一次，每個月擦掉甕身的鹽霜。

這一批：晴 124 日、陰 38 日、雨 18 日。

5

Step 5: Sun180 days

Lids off when it’s clear, on when the sky turns.

Read in English

Over six months the cloth is changed three times and the salt bloom wiped off every month.

This batch: 124 days of sun, 38 of cloud, 18 of rain.

第六道：慢火六小時

抽出的生醬用龍眼木柴火慢煮，邊煮邊撈浮沫，直到香氣由鹹轉甘。

6

Step 6: Slow firesix hours

The raw sauce simmers over longan wood, skimmed as it goes, until the smell turns from salt to caramel.
